1. General Overview & Headline Results

Campaign performance at a glance — verified souls, targets, spiritual outcomes & service distribution

Across Christ Embassy Abuja Zone 1, 24 groups and 127 churches mobilised soul winners to take the gospel to every corner of the FCT. This executive summary captures key results from the CEAZ1 RON tracker platform.

Leadership Perspective

With 18,961 souls recorded against a campaign goal of 50,000, CEAZ1 has achieved 37.9% of its overall zonal target. Zonal Church Group leads all groups with 6,479 souls (34.2% of the Zone), while churches across the zone including Zonal Church 2 (101.3%), CE Kuduru (328.0%), and CE KBS (109.3%) have surpassed their assigned individual church targets.
